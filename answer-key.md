## Classifier traps

Blank or generic `error_type` on 33% of failing steps · `context deadline exceeded` is a timeout · `503: upstream quota exhausted`
is a rate limit · `404 ... record ORD-… does not exist` is an invalid argument · `ValidationError` is invalid_tool_args on a
tool_call but output_parse_error on an output_check · 40% of guardrail blocks are refusals that complete with a failed check ·
half of `final_error_message` values are wrapped in `AgentRunError: step N (...) failed: …`.
